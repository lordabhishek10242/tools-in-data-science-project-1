## Examples of useful services (ideas, not restrictions)

1. **Your First Confused User:** Try a classmate's website without help; send a screen recording of where you get stuck.
2. **I'll Misread Your Chart:** Interpret a data visualization without explanation; tell the creator what you understood incorrectly.
3. **Test on My Device:** Test someone's app on your phone, browser, or connection and document failures.
4. **Skeptical Customer:** Listen to a three-minute product pitch, raise objections, and report what wasn't convincing.
5. **Eyes on the Ground:** Observe a safely accessible public location and report details not available online.
6. **Real-World Data Points:** Collect three local measurements with times, methods, and evidence.
7. **One Human Answer:** Ask a consenting person one neutral research question and share an anonymized response.
8. **Device Test Service:** Run requested tests on a device or environment someone else cannot access.
9. **Accountability Witness:** Check in on four agreed days and maintain a shared progress log.
10. **Mock Interview:** Ask unexpected follow-ups in a live interview and share specific feedback.
11. **Explain It to Me:** Listen to a difficult explanation, repeat what you understood, and identify confusion.
12. **Find My Blind Spot:** Try to disprove one assumption in a classmate's project with a concrete counterexample.
13. **Reproduce My Bug:** Reproduce a public software bug in a clean environment and report the minimal failure.
14. **Consent-Based Introduction:** Ask a relevant contact if they want an introduction and connect willing parties.
15. **Paired Problem-Solving:** Spend twenty minutes working together on a concrete problem and record next steps.

A service may be powered by agents, but ask what makes it worth *another student's credits*. Keep your offer specific and feasible. Do not offer credentials, private data, unsafe activities, or contact third parties without consent. The marketplace itself will launch separately; **you do not need to deliver or trade this week**.
